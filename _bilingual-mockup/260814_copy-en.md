## The shift

*Why another AI training won't fix it*

## Everyone bought the tools. Almost nobody changed how they work.

Generic training shows what AI can do, and a month later nobody is using it. The gap was never the tool, it's judgment: people plan, design, connect. Agents collect, interpret, execute, display. Once that judgment is in place, people know what to ask for, what to expect back, and when they need to step in and correct it. We start from the work the team already does, and everyone leaves with something built for their own job.
